About the Project

This website analyzes ambient air-quality trends using open environmental data collected from multiple strategic locations across the Boisar / Tarapur region in Palghar district, Maharashtra. As one of Western India's prominent industrial estates (MIDC) situated adjacent to residential, institutional, and coastal zones, Boisar presents a vital case study for environmental data science.

Users can select a specific monitoring location to examine Air Quality Index (AQI) trajectories and individual pollutant concentrations (PM2.5, PM10, NO₂, SO₂, and CO) across the monsoon period covering June, July, and August through interactive, dynamic data visualizations.
